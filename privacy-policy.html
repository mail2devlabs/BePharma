Your Privacy Matters

We respect your privacy and aim to handle information responsibly. This policy describes the general practices applicable to information submitted through the BePharma Traders website.

01

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide to us.

Name, business or organization information.

Email address and phone number when you contact us.

Product enquiry details and business requirements.

Technical information such as browser or device information where applicable.

02

How We Use Information

Information provided through the website may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate with you and improve our website and business services.

Respond to product and service enquiries.

Provide requested business information or quotations.

Maintain and improve website functionality.

Protect the website against misuse or unauthorized activity.

03

Enquiry & Contact Information

When you submit an enquiry or contact BePharma Traders, the information you provide may be used to understand your requirements and respond to your request.

If you use enquiry or communication features on the website, relevant details may be retained for business communication, follow-up and record-keeping purposes.

04

Cookies & Website Data

The website may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies to support website functionality and remember certain preferences.

Some technical information may also be collected automatically by hosting, analytics or other website technologies, where such services are used.

05

Information Sharing

BePharma Traders does not intend to sell personal information submitted through the website.

Information may be shared with relevant service providers or business partners when reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to enquiries, provide requested services or comply with legal requirements.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website and business communication processes.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through public website forms.

07

BePharma Traders does not control the privacy practices, policies or content of independent third-party websites and services. Users should review the privacy policies of those services where appropriate.

BePharma Traders may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the website, services, technologies or business practices.
